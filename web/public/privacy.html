Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: Senior uses what you type and the code you write to coach you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. A few trusted services process it so the app can work, and they are named below.

Who we are

Senior is a coding-interview coach built as a hackathon project at the Imperial AI Hack. It is a prototype, not a commercial service. You can contact us through our GitHub page.

What we collect

  • Your account, if you sign in. With Google sign-in we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google. With email sign-in, your email address. Guests are not asked for any of this.
  • What you send the coach. Your messages, your code, your test results and the hints you receive.
  • How the agents answered. A record of each step the AI agents take: which agent acted, which model or tool it used, tokens, cost, timing, the decision and its reason, and the first 500 characters of what went in and came out. This is the Activity log and the Agent inspector.
  • Optional features. Answers you give in the Profile interview, a job description and CV you paste into the CV studio, the name you type for a voice call, and the audio of a voice call while it is live.

How we use it

  • To give hints, run mock interviews and recommend roles.
  • To hand a stuck session to a human mentor, who sees the transcript and your code.
  • To show you, in the Agent inspector and Activity log, exactly what the agents did in your sessions and why.
  • To find and fix problems with the app.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your sessions

  • Other visitors cannot. They cannot read your messages, your code or your mentor tickets. The Activity log and the Mentor page show each visitor their own sessions only.
  • Mentors on the Senior team can. They can read every session: the messages, the code, what was entered in the Profile interview and the CV studio, and the record of what the agents did. This is how they answer a stuck session and fix problems.
  • Everyone sees a few totals for the whole system: how many actions the agents have taken, how many model and tool calls, and the spend. These are numbers only. They contain nothing you typed.
  • How Senior knows a session is yours. Each session has a long random id that works like a key. Your browser keeps the ids of the sessions you start, in its local storage, and sends them with each request. Anyone who has a session's id can open that session, so do not share one. Senior's own screens show only the last four characters of an id. On a shared computer, the next person to use the same browser can see those sessions until this site's data is cleared. Once it is cleared and Senior's tabs are closed, the list is gone and the sessions no longer appear for you either.

Services that process your data

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, hosting, the server and the stored activity log.
  • Anthropic (Claude): the AI models that read your messages and code and write the replies.
  • Linkup: web search for the Profile feature. It receives search queries built from your answers, not your account details.
  • ElevenLabs: the voice coach, only when you start a call.

How long we keep it

Sessions, chats and mentor tickets are kept on the server only while it is running and are cleared when it restarts. The record of agent actions (the Activity log) is kept until we delete it, and we will delete it when the hackathon project ends. Your sign-in account stays until you ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Use Senior as a guest, without an account.
  • Do not paste anything into Senior that you would not want an AI model and a mentor to read: passwords, or personal details beyond what a CV normally contains.
  • Ask us to see or delete your data through our GitHub page.

Children

Senior is meant for people aged 16 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
