In short: Senior is a free hackathon prototype. Use it to practise, be kind to mentors, and do not rely on it for anything that matters without checking.

What Senior is

Senior is a coding-interview coach built at the Imperial AI Hack. It gives graded hints instead of answers, runs mock interviews, and can hand you to a human mentor. It is a prototype offered free of charge and as it is.

Using Senior

  • You can use Senior as a guest or sign in with Google or email. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.
  • Use it to learn and practise. Do not use it to cheat in a real interview, test or exam.
  • Do not try to break, overload or misuse the service, or get the agents to produce harmful content.
  • Be respectful to human mentors.

AI output

Hints, interview feedback, role recommendations and tailored CVs are written by AI models and can be wrong. Check anything important before you act on it. Role recommendations come from public web pages, which we link so that you can check them.

Your content

What you write in Senior stays yours. You give us permission to process it to run the service, as the privacy policy describes.
